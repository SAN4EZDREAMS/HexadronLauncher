## 9. Notes on the choice

Kept here rather than in the licence text, because it is explanation and not
terms.

**Why not MIT, Apache-2.0 or GPL.** All three permit commercial use — GPL
included; copyleft restricts *closing the source*, not selling. None of them can
express "free for everyone, not for sale", which is the whole of what this
project wants.

**Relation to PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0.** That is the standard,
lawyer-drafted licence for exactly this intent, and the terms above are close to
it in substance. Anyone who prefers a vetted text over a bespoke one can read it
at <https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0/>; the Author may
adopt it later, and the notices in the source files would then name it instead.

**This is not legal advice.** These terms were drafted to match the Author's
stated intent, in plain language, by somebody who is not a lawyer. If the
licence ever has to be enforced, or if a company asks for something in writing,
have it reviewed by one.
